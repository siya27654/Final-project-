Simple Interest Calculator

Project Description

The Simple Interest Calculator is a simple application that calculates the interest earned on a principal amount over a specified period of time.

Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Where:

- Principal is the initial amount of money.
- Rate is the annual interest rate in percentage.
- Time is the period for which the money is invested or borrowed, usually in years.

Example

If the principal amount is ₹10,000, the interest rate is 5% per year, and the time period is 2 years:

Simple Interest = (10,000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = ₹1,000

Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest = ₹11,000

Features

- Calculates simple interest quickly.
- Easy to use.
- Accepts principal amount, interest rate, and time as inputs.
- Displays the calculated interest and total amount.

Technologies Used

- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript

How to Use

1. Enter the principal amount.
2. Enter the annual interest rate.
3. Enter the time period in years.
4. Click the Calculate button.
5. The simple interest and total amount will be displayed.
